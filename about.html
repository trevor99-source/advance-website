About Advance

Painting and epoxy flooring specialists, working across Gauteng since 2015.

Specialists in the surfaces your business stands on.


Since 2015, Advance has installed epoxy floors and painted properties for commercial, industrial and residential clients. Our work covers warehouses, factories, garages, showrooms, commercial spaces and homes.

From anti-slip and chemical-resistant floor finishes to industrial coatings for machinery and equipment, and interior and exterior painting, one team can look after the whole surface.

Our mission

To deliver durable, well-finished floors and painted surfaces for businesses and homes across Gauteng and beyond.

Flooring

Epoxy floors for warehouses, factories, garages, showrooms, commercial spaces and homes.

Coatings

Anti-slip, chemical-resistant and industrial coatings for floors, machinery and equipment.

Painting

Interior and exterior painting for commercial and residential properties.
