Frequently asked questions

Epoxy flooring, preparation, curing, durability, maintenance and coating options.

How should the floor be prepared?

Preparation depends on the condition of your existing surface and the system chosen. Tell us about the floor and send photos, and we will advise what is needed before installation.

Does the area need to be cleared?

Yes, the area generally needs to be accessible and clear. We will confirm what to move when we agree the schedule.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the size of the area, the system and site conditions. We confirm the timeline in your quote.

Can I use a warehouse or factory floor during installation?

Access during work depends on the project. Tell us how your site operates and we will plan around it where possible.

When can the floor be used?

Curing times vary by product and conditions. We will give you the time for foot traffic, vehicles and heavy equipment when we quote.

How durable is an epoxy floor?

Durability depends on the system, preparation and how the floor is used. We recommend a finish suited to your traffic and chemical exposure.

How do I look after the floor?

We provide care guidance for the finish we install, so you know how to clean it and keep it looking good.

What coating options are there?

We offer epoxy floor systems, anti-slip and chemical-resistant finishes, industrial coatings for machinery and equipment, and interior and exterior paint.

Can you make a floor anti-slip or chemical-resistant?

Yes. Anti-slip and chemical-resistant finishes are part of our service. Tell us where the floor is used so we can recommend the right one.

Use the quote form, call +27 61 727 9631 or message us on WhatsApp with your location, property type, service and approximate area.
